# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Participation must remain respectful regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private correspondence or personal data.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions without gatekeeping.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on scope while using issues and proposals to challenge them constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and bad-faith manipulation are not tolerated in project spaces or public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on the GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
